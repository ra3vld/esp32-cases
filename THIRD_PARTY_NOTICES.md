## Reference dimensions

The CrowPanel reference is an original simplified model made with analytic primitives from measured overall dimensions and mounting coordinates. No surfaces or bodies from the manufacturer's detailed STEP are included. The reference preserves the 79 mm front diameter, the front/back envelope and the three M3 mounting axes. Connector bodies are represented by simple clearance envelopes.

Manufacturer information: https://github.com/Elecrow-RD/CrowPanel-2.1inch-HMI-ESP32-Rotary-Display-480-480-IPS-Round-Touch-Knob-Screen

The ESP32-3248S035 dimensions refer to the Jingcai family datasheet linked in `esp32-3248s035c/capsule-v01/sources.json`. The manufacturer's PDF is not bundled. The linked board-layout photograph is a reference only and is not bundled.
